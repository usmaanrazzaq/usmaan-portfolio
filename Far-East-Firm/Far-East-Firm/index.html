Far East Firm

Designing & developing a website for an architectural firm

Web Design Front-End Development

Overview

Far East Firm is a firm specializing in architectural visualization and design services. They provide comprehensive drafting and 3D modeling services for architectural projects, helping clients visualize their designs before construction.

Process

The design process focused on creating a professional, clean aesthetic that would appeal to architects, contractors, and property developers. I emphasized showcasing their portfolio and services while maintaining a sophisticated visual hierarchy that reflects the precision required in architectural work.
